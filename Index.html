L. Kalaidhoo, Laamu Atoll

A quiet inhabited island where fishing boats, evening football and friendly neighbours set the pace. You stay among the community, not behind a resort fence.

House reef

A 428 m living reef with a sharp drop-off. Snorkel gear and a dive rinse station are included.

Yin Yang surf break

One of Laamu's best-known waves. Board storage on site; we help arrange boat trips and check swell.

Sandbanks

Isolated white-sand banks for private picnics, reachable by local dhoni.

Bikini Beach

A two-minute stroll from the Sunset Villa for swimming and sunsets.

Getting here

We coordinate the whole journey. Send your flight details when you book and we'll meet you on arrival.

  1. Fly to Velana International (MLE)

    Land in Malé on your international flight.

  2. Domestic flight to Kadhdhoo (KDO)

    A scenic atoll flight of about 35 minutes.

  3. Ride to Holiday Villa

    About 20 minutes by coastal causeway or speedboat. Your host meets you at the airport.

Transfer price

Per person, round trip, Malé to Holiday Villa. Add it during booking.
